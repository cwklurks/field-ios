! Field's own block rules, in Adblock Plus syntax, added to the first domain list.
! Generic ad scripts by name, first party or third: bait files like these are
! what ad-block tests and many ad loaders use.
/ads.js^$script
/pagead.js^$script
! Ad hosts the domain lists leave to EasyList for its exceptions (domains.py)
! that EasyList doesn't block well itself. A block here can't be undone by
! EasyList's exceptions, so each is third party only and spares the sites
! those exceptions are for.
! Ad platforms' own ad and ad-API hosts: their portals are same-site, and X
! is moving from twitter.com to x.com, so each spares the other.
||ads-api.twitter.com^$third-party,domain=~x.com
||ads.x.com^$third-party,domain=~twitter.com
||ads.tiktok.com^$third-party
||ads.pinterest.com^$third-party
||gemini.yahoo.com^$third-party
||afs.googlesyndication.com^$third-party
! Ad servers and ad networks whose exceptions are for their own pages.
||ad.atown.jp^$third-party
||adfurikun.jp^$third-party
||adnetmedia.lt^$third-party
||infotop.jp^$third-party
! Ad and push networks whose exceptions are for one site's player or
! sign-up frame: blocked everywhere but there.
||optimatic.com^$third-party,domain=~pch.com
||worldgravity.com^$third-party,domain=~hotstar.com|~jiohotstar.com
||mycleverpush.com^$third-party,domain=~bsdex.de|~heise.de
